## Notice to all HBI team members and agents

This document previously proposed CKER as the top-level framing for HBI knowledge architecture. The Product Owner has since clarified and decided that this framing is not the HBI top-level architecture and must not be used to direct the design or create additional repositories, layers, approval gates or workflows.

**Do not implement or extend CKER as a separate architecture initiative. Do not treat the former CKER flow, its proposed separation of Knowledge and Evidence, or its additional claim-level QA framing as mandatory product-intake stages.**

The current and authoritative architecture is the three-part HBI model:

1. **ProductKnowledge** — one bank of product information researched to the PO's stated standards and approved by the PO before entry.
2. **Customer Context** — the customer's relevant profile, history, current condition and consultation need.
3. **Intelligence & Response** — the system's analysis of the connection between the product information and the customer's context, producing an appropriate response.

Evidence, source references and quality checks may support the collection and reliability of ProductKnowledge. They are not separate top-level architecture pillars, parallel product banks or extra approval barriers after the PO's acceptance.

The previous CKER text is retained only as a historical record of a superseded proposal. In case of any conflict, follow [HBI-PO-DEC-THREE-PART-001](./HBI-PO-DEC-THREE-PART-001.md).
