## In scope (conceptual)

## Out of scope

Medical diagnosis; allergy diagnosis; medical clearance; pregnancy-related medical advice; universal safety guarantee.

## Rules

```text
Customer discomfort = report, not diagnosis
Documented allergen declaration ≠ medical clearance
No disclosed allergen data ≠ no risk
```

When discomfort is reported during sampling: stop; record; do not diagnose; escalate per approved policy when available.
