# HBI Customer Reality Evidence Plan V0.1

## Purpose

This Plan defines the evidence boundary and minimum evidence required to start and then assess Customer Reality.

It does not create a Customer Corpus, extract or accept Needs, expand the Problem Bank, or change Recommendation.

---

## Evidence Levels

Customer Reality in this Plan has two explicitly separate levels.

### L1 — Start Reality

**Goal**

Prove that Customer Reality has actually started with at least one real, traceable evidence item.

**Minimum Evidence**

At least one of:

1. One real **Customer Statement** with valid, traceable provenance; or
2. One real **Encounter** with valid, traceable provenance.

**L1 is sufficient only to establish:**

> Customer Reality has started / real evidence is present.

**L1 is not sufficient to establish:**

- Problem Bank sufficiency
- coverage of the Problem Bank
- completeness of Customer Reality
- statistical or operational representation of the Gallery
- Need Bank sufficiency

Therefore:

> **L1 = Start Evidence, not Coverage Validation**

---

### L2 — Assess Bank Coverage

**Goal**

Assess whether the current Problem Bank provides usable coverage against a bounded set of real and varied consultation Encounters.

**Minimum Evidence**

Multiple **real and varied Encounters** with valid, traceable provenance and sufficient context for comparison.

The evidence must provide enough variation to permit bounded assessment of outcomes such as:

- Covered
- Covered but consultation role unclear
- Missing Problem candidate
- Outside current scope
- Safety / referral boundary
- Unclear / insufficient evidence

**L2 may support:**

> An evidence-bounded assessment of whether the current Problem Bank covers the reviewed Encounter Slice sufficiently.

L2 does not establish complete coverage of all Customer Reality, all customers, or the whole Gallery. Conclusions remain bounded by the Evidence and Encounter Slice actually reviewed.

---

## Non-Interpretable Rules

The following rules are mandatory:

> **One real Encounter ≠ validation of the whole Problem Bank.**

> **L1 ≠ L2.**

> **Customer Reality exists ≠ Problem Bank sufficiency is proven.**

A single real Encounter must not be interpreted as evidence that the whole Problem Bank is Covered, complete, sufficient, or ready for Need Extraction.

---

## Evidence Boundary

Evidence used for L1 or L2 should have, where applicable:

- Known Origin
- Traceable Provenance
- Preserved Context
- Permission / Consent Boundary
- Separation of Customer Statement from Operator Interpretation

The following must remain distinct:

- Customer-originated language
- Operator Interpretation
- Clinical Interpretation
- System-derived Hypothesis
- Synthetic / Generated Language

**Synthetic Customer Language is not Customer Reality Evidence.**

Hypothesized wording must not silently become Customer Language evidence.

Customer statement ≠ operator interpretation ≠ system inference ≠ clinical evidence.

---

## Progression Rule

```text
No Verified Evidence
        ↓
L1 — Start Reality
        ↓
Additional Real + Diverse Encounters
        ↓
L2 — Assess Bank Coverage
```

Progression from L1 to L2 is not automatic.

L1 only establishes that Reality Evidence has started.

L2 is considered only when the available evidence has sufficient volume and variation for a bounded comparison against the Problem Bank.

---

## Problem Bank Boundary

### At L1

- Problem Bank does not change.
- No new Problem is added merely because one Encounter is observed.
- No Problem is promoted to canonical status from L1 evidence alone.

### At L2

If real Evidence identifies a concrete Gap, that Gap must first be recorded as an **evidence-backed finding** with its supporting Encounter/Evidence.

An observed case does not by itself authorize free expansion of the Problem Bank.

Any later Bank revision requires its own review, verification, and authorization.

---

## Explicit Non-Scope

This Plan does not authorize:

- Bank Expansion
- Map Change
- Need Extraction
- Need Acceptance
- Problem → Need → Product mapping
- Recommendation Change
- Scoring Change
- Diagnosis Taxonomy Expansion
- Questionnaire Implementation
- Schema Change
- API Change
- Coding
- Product/Recommendation implementation
- Merge

---

## State Interpretation

```text
Customer Reality = NOT VERIFIED
        ↓
L1 Evidence obtained
        ↓
Customer Reality = STARTED / EVIDENCE PRESENT
        ↓
L2 Evidence threshold satisfied
        ↓
Bank Coverage Assessment permitted
```

None of these states means:

```text
Need Ready
Product Ready
Recommendation Ready
Implementation Ready
```

---

## Exit Criteria

### L1 Exit

At least one valid Evidence item is available with provenance and context sufficient for independent checking.

### L2 Entry

Multiple real and varied Encounters are available with provenance and context sufficient for bounded comparison with the Problem Bank.

### L2 Exit

For the reviewed Encounter Slice, the principal outcomes are explicitly classified, for example:

```text
Covered
Covered but consultation role unclear
Missing
Outside Scope
Safety / Referral Boundary
Unclear / Insufficient Evidence
```

Every claimed Gap must have identifiable supporting Evidence.

L2 does not authorize Problem Bank expansion; it only permits an evidence-backed coverage finding.

---

## Governance Rule

This Plan must always distinguish:

```text
Reality Exists
        ≠
Reality Verified
        ≠
Problem Bank Coverage Assessed
        ≠
Problem Bank Accepted as Sufficient
```

Therefore:

> **Start Reality is not Coverage Validation.**
